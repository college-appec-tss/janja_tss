About Our School

Developing skilled, responsible and productive citizens through technical and vocational education.

Janja Technical Secondary School

Janja Technical Secondary School, also known as Janja TVET School or Janja T.S.S, is located in Busengo Sector, Gakenke District, Northern Province, Rwanda.

Our Catholic-affiliated, government-aided boarding institution is committed to technical and vocational education, practical skills, discipline and responsibility.

We prepare students to contribute to national development through professional skills, creativity and entrepreneurship.
